# Disclaimer

**ClearAnswer is a personal, educational demonstration project.**

1. **No real data.** Every member, provider, claim, EOB, and health plan in this
   repository is synthetic and fictional. No PHI was used at any point.

2. **Simplified reference content.** Adjustment-code explanations are consumer-level
   simplifications of public X12 code meanings; the "Sunrise Health PPO" plan and its
   rules are fictional; rights summaries are educational, not legal advice. None of it
   is authoritative for any real claim.

3. **Not billing, legal, or medical advice.** ClearAnswer explains documents and flags
   patterns worth verifying. It never determines what anyone actually owes and never
   instructs payment. Real billing disputes should go through your plan, your
   provider's billing office, and where applicable, official appeal processes.

4. **Not affiliated.** This is a personal project built on personal time with public
   information. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or based on any employer's
   data, processes, or systems, nor any payer or provider organization.

5. **No warranty.** Provided "as is" under the MIT license. Model outputs can be
   wrong; the eval suite quantifies performance on a small synthetic set and is not a
   claim of production-grade accuracy.
